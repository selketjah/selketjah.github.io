At [KanDDDinsky](https://kandddinsky.com/), [Kenny Baas-Schwegler](https://bsky.app/profile/kenny.weave-it.org) and I were invited to give our talk on the relationship between (sub)domains, bounded contexts and service boundaries. I think the talk fills an absence in the discussion around domains vs bounded contexts. We have been discussing this for a few years now, sharing our ideas and opinions on it, so it is very nice to see its fruits.
